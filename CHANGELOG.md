# Changelog

All notable changes to WipTracker are documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-18

### Added

- **The task stack shows its depth.** In the stack window the top task is drawn bigger,
  and the names fade towards the dim tone the deeper they sit.
- **A light palette.** _light colours_ in the menu switches the bar and every window to
  a bright look; _dark colours_ switches back.
- **Shuffled colours.** _shuffle colours_ tints the whole palette with a random hue —
  contrast stays as it was — and _reset colours_ returns the stock look. Works on the
  dark and the light palette alike.
